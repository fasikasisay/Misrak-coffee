Welcome to Misrak coffee

Our Story

Misrak coffee was founded in 2010 with the mission to provide high-quality, ethically sourced coffee to our customers. We believe in supporting local farmers and promoting sustainable practices in the coffee industry.

Our Coffee

We offer a wide variety of coffee blends, including single-origin beans from around the country. Our coffee is roasted to perfection to bring out the unique flavors and aromas of each blend.

Harar

A rich and full-bodied coffee from the Harar region of Ethiopia, known for its fruity and floral notes.

yrgachefe

A smooth and well-balanced coffee from the Yrgachefe region of Ethiopia, known for its bright acidity and complex flavor profile.

nekemte

A vibrant and complex coffee from the Nekemte region of Ethiopia, known for its bright acidity and fruity notes.
